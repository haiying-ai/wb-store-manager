# Pricing Rules

Profitability is a hard gate.

Required pricing inputs:

- product cost
- cost currency and exchange rate when needed
- first-mile freight or explicit zero
- fulfillment cost
- WB commission rate
- planned price
- planned discount
- payment fee
- ad reserve
- tax
- target net margin

Do not treat source-material price as planned sale price unless the user explicitly confirms it. If a review fails the margin gate, the SKU must not be listed until pricing is fixed.
